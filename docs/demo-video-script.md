Record the live website, not mock screens. The RealPage presenter allocated 75 points to extraction, address coverage, citations and change tracking, and 25 to plain language, responsible design and scale. This sequence shows those behaviors without claiming a private test score.

**0:00–0:15 — One address**

“RealPage asked a difficult question: for this property, which rental rules apply today, and what changes next? DwellingOS turns the supplied 500 properties and legal corpus into source-backed passports.” Open the **Know when not to guess** sample, `A0013`.

**0:15–0:40 — Responsible answer**

"Newark's cited rule has a possible 4% ceiling, but this building's rent-control status is missing. We do not guess. The next operator action is to verify that specific fact." Point to the decision brief and source, then click **Explore a hypothetical fact** and choose **Hypothetically covered**. Show the changed result and the unsaved-scenario warning. The actual building remains unverified.

**0:40–1:00 — Correct jurisdiction**

Open **Mailing city ≠ legal city**. Show Dorchester resolving to Boston and the Census address-range status. Explicitly say city-law matches remain provisional until the parcel boundary is verified; neither a postal alias nor ZIP proves it.

**1:00–1:20 — Time and source**

Open **Before versus after**. Point to the California algorithmic-rent rule as `not yet effective` on 2025-12-31, then click **Show after date** in the passport and show `applies` on 2026-01-02. Expand full rule evidence for its exact citation and effective date.

**1:20–1:40 — Change impact**

Open **One law, many properties** and T3. Show the NJ FAIR Act's 140 candidate sample addresses and 90 possible local conflicts. Open one address and the before/after spotlight. Say “possible” clearly; unresolved preemption is not a definitive legal conclusion.

**1:40–2:00 — Technical path and close**

“This is not 500 handwritten answers. Source ingestion, reviewed rule extraction, an auditable Python decision engine, and indexed portfolio queries drive the demo. We benchmarked the data path on 20 million synthetic rows; real deployment still needs verified property boundaries, complete law coverage and expert legal review.” End on the workflow/scale panel and `Not legal advice` notice.
